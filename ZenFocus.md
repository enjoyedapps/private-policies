# Privacy Policy for ZenFocus

**Effective Date:** October 22, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 22, 2026

## Overview
ZenFocus ("we", "our", or "the app") is a standalone, offline productivity and Pomodoro timer application designed to help individuals maintain deep focus and organize work/study sessions.

Your privacy is our utmost priority. ZenFocus is built with a **Privacy-First** architecture.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use
ZenFocus does **not** collect, transmit, sell, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) or device data. 

- **No Account Required:** You do not need to register, log in, or provide an email address to use any feature of this app.
- **No Third-Party Analytics:** We do not embed Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Facebook SDK, or any third-party behavioral trackers.
- **No Advertising SDKs:** The application is 100% ad-free. No advertising identifiers (AAID) are accessed or tracked.

---

## 2. Local Data Storage
All data generated during your usage of ZenFocus—including timer configuration preferences, focus session counts, and activity logs—is stored **exclusively on your local device** using Android's local Room database and DataStore.

- We do not possess servers, databases, or cloud instances capable of accessing or intercepting your local data.
- If you uninstall the application or clear application data via Android system settings, all locally stored logs and preferences will be permanently deleted.

---

## 3. Device Permissions
ZenFocus requests only the minimal system permissions required for core timer functionality:

- **VIBRATE (`android.permission.VIBRATE`):** Used solely to trigger haptic feedback when a focus or break interval timer finishes.
- **POST_NOTIFICATIONS (`android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS`):** Used solely on Android 13 (API 33) and above to display a local notification when your timer finishes while the app is in the background.

ZenFocus **never** requests sensitive permissions such as Camera, Microphone, Precise Location, Contacts, SMS, or External Storage access.

---

## 4. Children's Privacy
ZenFocus is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any data from children under the age of 13. Because the app collects no information whatsoever, it is compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and GDPR.
